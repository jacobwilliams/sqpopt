      40              : !      be far away, or infinite, and a round-off multiplier of the wrong sign
      41              : !      is then not a large violation); equality constraints are free);
      42              : !    - primal feasibility of the constraints and variable bounds;
      43              : !    - with `dual_inf_tol` and `f_scale`, stationarity of the *unscaled*
      44              : !      problem (the scaled residual divided by `f_scale`) to within
      45              : !      `dual_inf_tol`: with automatic scaling, `ktol` applies to the scaled
      46              : !      problem, which can be very loose in the original units.
      47              : !

      49              : !    `ktol*s_d`, where \( s_d = \max(s_{max}, \lVert\lambda\rVert_1/m)/s_{max} \)
