       5              : !  Direct minimum-norm solves with rows of the constraint Jacobian
       6              : !  (`options%direct_least_squares`), by a sparse factorization (see
       7              : !  [[sqpopt_kkt_module]]; MUMPS or QDLDL, see `options%linear_solver`).
       8              : !
       9              : !  The Gauss-Newton restoration step ([[restoration_step]]) and the
      10              : !  second-order correction ([[soc_step]]) both need the minimum-norm
      11              : !  solution `d` of
      12              : !
      13              : !  $$ J_S\, d = r $$
      14              : !
      15              : !  for a set `S` of the Jacobian's rows (in the least-squares sense, if the
      16              : !  rows are dependent or the system is inconsistent). Without this module
      17              : !  they use the iterative solver `LSQR`, and take no step if it stops at its
      18              : !  iteration limit. Here the solution comes from one factorization and one
      19              : !  solve of
      20              : !
      21              : !  $$ \begin{bmatrix} I & J_S^T \\ J_S & -\epsilon I \end{bmatrix}
      22              : !     \begin{bmatrix} d \\ y \end{bmatrix} =
      23              : !     \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ r \end{bmatrix} $$
      24              : !
      25              : !  whose solution is \( d = J_S^T (J_S J_S^T + \epsilon I)^{-1} r \). The
      26              : !  small regularization \( \epsilon \) (relative to the square of the
      27              : !  largest element of \( J_S \)) keeps the matrix nonsingular when the rows
      28              : !  are dependent; as \( \epsilon \to 0 \), `d` tends to the minimum-norm
      29              : !  least-squares solution.
      30              : !
      31              : !  The same matrix, with another right-hand side, gives the least-squares
      32              : !  *multiplier estimate* \( \lambda = \arg\min \lVert g - J_S^T \lambda
      33              : !  \rVert \) of [[multiplier_estimate]], which [[sqpopt_iterate]] uses
      34              : !  when the QP's multipliers can't be trusted. That routine works in every
      35              : !  build: it uses `LSQR` unless this module's direct solver is enabled.
