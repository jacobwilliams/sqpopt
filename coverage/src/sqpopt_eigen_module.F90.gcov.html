       5              : !  Eigenvalues and eigenvectors of small dense symmetric matrices, for the
       6              : !  spectral decomposition of the limited-memory Hessians (see
       7              : !  [[sqpopt_spectral_module]], whose matrices are of the order of the
       8              : !  number of stored pairs).
       9              : !
      10              : !  Three methods:
      11              : !
      12              : !  * `sqpopt_eigen_ql`: Householder reduction to tridiagonal form (with the
      13              : !    reflections accumulated), then the implicit QL method with Wilkinson's
      14              : !    shift (the algorithm of EISPACK's `tred2`/`tql2`), always available,
      15              : !    in any real kind. About \( 4n^3/3 \) operations for the eigenvalues,
      16              : !    and \( 6n^3 \) or so with the eigenvectors.
      17              : !  * `sqpopt_eigen_jacobi`: the cyclic Jacobi method (plane rotations that
      18              : !    zero each off-diagonal element in turn, sweep after sweep, with
      19              : !    Rutishauser's threshold strategy), always available. The simplest and
      20              : !    the most accurate (small eigenvalues to high relative accuracy), but
      21              : !    about \( 20 n^3 \) operations.
      22              : !  * `sqpopt_eigen_lapack`: LAPACK's `DSYEV` (the same algorithm as
      23              : !    `sqpopt_eigen_ql`, in blocked code), only in a library built with
      24              : !    LAPACK (the `HAS_LAPACK` preprocessor directive, in double precision).
      25              : !
      26              : !  `sqpopt_eigen_auto` picks LAPACK where it is available, and
      27              : !  `sqpopt_eigen_ql` otherwise. (On the Hock-Schittkowski problems with the
      28              : !  convexified SR1 matrix, whose eigenproblems have orders up to 100,
      29              : !  the eigensolver's time was 3.4 s with Jacobi and 0.6 s with LAPACK.)
