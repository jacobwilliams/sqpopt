       5              : !  The spectral decomposition of the limited-memory Hessian approximations
       6              : !  (BFGS and SR1), from their compact representation, without forming an
       7              : !  `n x n` matrix (Burdakov, Gratton, Toint, Zikrin 2017; Brust, Burdakov,
       8              : !  Erway, Marcia, *Algorithm 1030: SC-SR1*, ACM TOMS 48(4), 2022, section 2.3).
       9              : !
      10              : !  The matrix is \( B = \theta I + U C U^T \), with \( \theta \) the initial
      11              : !  scaling plus the shift, `U` the `n x r` low-rank factor (`r` = `k` for SR1,
      12              : !  `2k` for BFGS, for `k` stored pairs) and \( C = \sigma M^{-1} \) the small
      13              : !  middle matrix (see [[sqpopt_hessian_module]]). With the eigendecomposition
      14              : !  of the Gram matrix \( U^TU = V D V^T \), keeping the `q` eigenvalues that
      15              : !  are not negligible, \( U = Q R \) with \( Q = U V D^{-1/2} \) orthonormal
      16              : !  (a thin QR factorization, from the stored inner products only) and
      17              : !  \( R = D^{1/2} V^T \). Then
      18              : !
      19              : !  $$ B = \theta I + Q (R C R^T) Q^T, \qquad R C R^T = W \hat\Lambda W^T $$
      20              : !
      21              : !  so the eigenvalues of `B` are \( \theta + \hat\lambda_i \) (`q` of them),
      22              : !  with the eigenvectors \( P = Q W = U (V D^{-1/2} W) \), and \( \theta \)
      23              : !  with multiplicity `n - q` (every vector orthogonal to `P`). It costs two
      24              : !  symmetric eigenproblems of order at most `r` (see [[sqpopt_eigen_module]])
      25              : !  and no operation on vectors of size `n`. The eigenvectors are never
      26              : !  formed: only the `r x q` matrix \( V D^{-1/2} W \) is kept, and products
      27              : !  with `P` are products with `U` (`O(nr)` each), so a trust-region step
      28              : !  costs about `4nr` operations, as many as an L-BFGS product.
      29              : !
      30              : !  Two uses (see [[sqpopt_iterate_module]] and [[sqpopt_qp_solver_module]]):
      31              : !  the smallest eigenvalue, to shift an indefinite SR1 matrix to a positive
      32              : !  definite one before a QP (`hessian%convexify`), and the minimizer of the
      33              : !  QP's objective within the step cap, an L2 trust-region subproblem solved
      34              : !  in closed form in the eigenvector basis (the unconstrained step of the
      35              : !  SR1 matrix).
