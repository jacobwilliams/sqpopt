       6              : !  and SR1 (`options%inertia_control`): the shift \( \delta \) of
       7              : !  \( H + \delta I \) is found from a sparse factorization of the KKT

      13              : !  The QP subproblem's step is only a minimizer if the Hessian is positive
      14              : !  definite on the null space of the working set's constraints,
      15              : !  \( Z^THZ \succ 0 \). The factorization of the KKT matrix tells how many
