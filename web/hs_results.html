Hock–Schittkowski test results

Every one of the 305 problems in Schittkowski's collection, solved by SQPOPT from its standard starting point, next to the published results of Schittkowski's NLPQLP, and to SLSQP run on the same problems. A problem is solved if the final point is feasible and its objective is within the tolerance of the validated optimum (or better), local if the solver converged to a feasible point with a worse objective (a different local solution), and failed otherwise. See the Performance section of the guide for the other configurations.
